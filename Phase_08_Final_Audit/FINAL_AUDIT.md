# Phase 8 - final audit and submission readiness

## What was audited, and how

`verification/final_consistency.py` re-derives every cross-artifact fact from the artifacts
themselves rather than trusting one document. It reads the two Markdown sources, the two
`.tex` files, the `.bib`, the `.docx`, the two PDFs, `SUPPLEMENTARY_TABLES.xlsx`,
`MANUSCRIPT_STATS.json`, `REF_ORDER.json` / `REFERENCES_ORDERED.json`,
`01_Literature/REFERENCES_VERIFIED.json`, all code-freeze manifests and
`07_Tests/TEST_RESULTS.json`, and it exits non-zero on any mismatch.
Machine output: `verification/final_consistency.json`.

    $ python3 Phase_08_Final_Audit/verification/final_consistency.py
      note  7 of the 9 headline constants are also quoted in the companion; the rest belong to the results tables and appear in the manuscript alone by design
      note  6 author-input placeholders remain (identity, funding, repository, CRediT, competing interest)
      note  15 path(s) uncommitted at audit time (they are committed at the end of the phase)

  cited at least once and nothing is cited that is not listed (no orphan, no dangling key);
  the `.bib` keys are exactly the printed keys; every `.bib` key is reached by a `\cite` in
  the manuscript `.tex`; the PDF carries reference numbers 1..55 on its 33 pages.
  `REF_ORDER.json` and `REFERENCES_ORDERED.json` agree with all of it, and the verification

  (`CODE_FREEZE_submission_2026_10_03j.json`), and all 27 of its entries hash-match the code

  exploratory)") matches `TEST_RESULTS.json`, whose status is COMPLETE with 28 cases; the

* **House style.** No banned hyperbole word and no project-internal vocabulary survives in

* **Gates.** `tools/audit_tex.py`, `tools/check_crossrefs.py`, `tools/check_tex_commands.py`,
