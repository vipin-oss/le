# Text rules for scholar-facing documents

Applies to every document handed to a scholar (Sumit, Neha, Anu, and any later set).

1. No document may refer to internal logs, audits, checklists, earlier errors, or project
   history. A reader who was not present must not be able to tell that anything was ever
   corrected.
2. No document may contain a derivation of anything the scholar is asked to check or
   redo. State the result, cite it or mark it as ours, and leave the working to the reader.
3. Review each new file against the two rules above before delivery, and re-check the
   existing sets when they are next touched.
