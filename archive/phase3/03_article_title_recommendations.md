## 1. Title Recommendations

### 1.1 Primary recommendation

**"Wave Propagation, Energy Transport, and Interface Phenomena in Generalized Multiphysics Continua: A Review"**

**Rationale:**
- Covers all Phase 2 scope areas (wave propagation, energy transport, interface phenomena)
- "Generalized multiphysics continua" encompasses thermoelastic, piezoelectric, porous, nonlocal, MDD, fractional theories
- "Review" signals the article type clearly
- Colon subtitle follows the reference model pattern (Abdalla et al. uses a descriptive title with colon)
- 13 words — within typical journal limits (most allow 15–20 words)

### 1.4 Recommended title for submission

**Title A** is recommended as primary because:
1. It most comprehensively covers the evidence base (all 24 Phase 2 records plus extended search)
2. "Interface phenomena" captures the RG corpus strength (all 7 RG studies are interface problems)
3. "Energy transport" captures the Phase 2 core contribution (energy flux framework)
4. "Generalized multiphysics continua" encompasses all theory families (LS, GN, DPL, TPL, MGT, MDD, nonlocal, piezoelectric, porous)
