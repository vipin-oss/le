## 4. Version A vs. Version B Strategy

### Version A — Comprehensive journal review (60-90 pages)

**Target journals:** JMPS, IJSS, CMT, Wave Motion

**Structure:**
- Condensed front matter (notation table only)
- Sections 1-3 (foundations): 15 pages
- Sections 4-7 (theories): 25 pages
- Sections 8-10 (methods, interfaces, energy): 25 pages
- Sections 11-12 (advanced, synthesis): 15 pages
- Sections 13-17 (applications, taxonomy, challenges, future, conclusions): 10 pages
- References: 10 pages
- Appendices: minimal or online supplementary

**Focus:** Core theories, key comparisons, main findings, essential figures (10-15), essential tables (8-10)

**Best for:** JMPS, IJSS, Wave Motion, CMT

### Version B — Extended authoritative review (100-150 pages + supplementary)

**Target journals:** Proceedings of the Royal Society A, or as monograph/special issue

**Structure:**
- Full front matter (notation + acronym tables)
- All 17 sections at full length
- All appendices (equation register, benchmark specs, figure specs, table specs)
- All figures (25-40)
- All tables (14-16)
- Extensive references (300+)

**Focus:** Complete treatment, exhaustive comparisons, full taxonomy, detailed roadmap, comprehensive supplementary material

**Best for:** Royal Society A (if invited), special issue, or monograph

## 6. Presubmission Inquiry Strategy

### When to inquire

Before submitting a full review, send presubmission inquiry to:

1. **JMPS** — editor-in-chief, asking about review article interest and length
2. **IJSS** — editor, asking about review scope and length
3. **CMT** — editor, asking about review fit

### Inquiry template

```
Dear Editor,

I am preparing a review article titled "Wave Propagation, Energy Transport, and Interface Phenomena in Generalized Multiphysics Continua: A Review."

The article provides:
- Historical synthesis of continuum wave theory from classical elasticity to generalized multiphysics formulations
- Multidimensional taxonomy classifying 150+ studies across physical, mathematical, wave, energy, and validation dimensions
- Comparative synthesis of 9 theory families (classical, LS, GL, GN II/III, DPL, TPL, MGT, MDD, nonlocal)
- Original research gap identification with evidence levels
- Future research roadmap organized by theory/computation/experiment

Estimated length: 60-80 pages (Version A) or 100-150 pages (Version B).
Estimated figures: 15-25 (Version A) or 25-40 (Version B).
Estimated references: 150-250.

The review is constructive and synthetic in tone, following the editorial philosophy of organizing and synthesizing established knowledge without adversarial fault-finding.

Would your journal be interested in considering this review? If so, what length and format would be most appropriate?

Sincerely,
[Author]
```

## 8. Recommended Submission Strategy

### Phase 1: Prepare Version A (60-90 pages)
- Focus on core theories, key comparisons, main findings
- 10-15 figures, 8-10 tables
- 150-200 references
- Prepare presubmission inquiry

### Phase 2: Presubmission inquiries (parallel)
- Send to JMPS, IJSS, CMT editors
- Gauge interest and length preferences

### Phase 3: Submit to first-choice journal
- Based on editor feedback
- If declined, revise and submit to next choice

### Phase 4: Version B as fallback
- If all journals decline Version A, expand to Version B
- Consider Royal Society A or special issue
