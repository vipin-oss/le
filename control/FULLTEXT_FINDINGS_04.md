## F13 GN numbering is OPPOSITE to Brave's GAP-04 wording
- GN 1993, J. Elasticity 31:189-208 ("Thermoelasticity without energy dissipation"; received 31 May 1991): this is thermoelasticity TYPE II. Printed: "referred to here as thermoelasticity type II, since it involves no dissipation of energy". Linearised type II: heat as waves at finite speed, entropy flux from the same potential as stress, no dissipation; integral identity (4.15) carries K* theta^2-type term.
- GN 1992, J. Thermal Stresses 15:253-264 ("On undamped heat waves in an elastic solid"): the body of the paper is thermoelasticity TYPE III (field eqs (3.19)-(3.20) carry both K lap(theta-dot-type) and K* lap(.) terms). Undamped waves arise in the type III system only when conductivity is small (K <-> K*/a0 comparison, eq 4.10).

## F14 Moore-Gibson 1960, J. Aerospace Sci. 27:117-127 (DOI 10.2514/8.8418)
- It is gas dynamics: generalised wave equation for sound in a gas with relaxation (molecular vibration/dissociation); third-order equation with attenuation and dispersion, discontinuous-front solutions. Not heat conduction in an elastic solid.
- Therefore "MGT thermoelasticity" (Quintanilla 2019 and followers) is an ANALOGUE that borrows the third-order structure; the review must not say Moore-Gibson 1960 derived a heat-conduction law. Cite it as origin of the third-order acoustic equation, and Thompson 1972 (not found) separately.
