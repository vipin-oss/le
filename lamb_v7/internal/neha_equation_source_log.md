## Critical read before any novelty sentence

Zhang, Song, Li, Han, Yu & Yang, Acta Mech. Solida Sin. (2026), 10.1007/s10338-026-00755-4, "Generalized
Thermoelastic Lamb Waves in One-Dimensional Hexagonal Quasicrystal Nanoplates". Crossref gave the title and
authors only; the full text was not reachable. This paper may already contain the heat operator we propose.
Week one of Paper B is to obtain it and write down: which generalized thermoelastic model it uses, whether
it has one lag or two, whether it reports attenuation, and whether the phason is dynamic or static. Until
that is answered, the novelty claim in the plan is provisional and says so.
