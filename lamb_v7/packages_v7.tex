\noindent\textbf{What is in the delivery.} \texttt{deliver/Lamb\_Sumit.zip},
\texttt{deliver/Lamb\_Neha.zip}, \texttt{deliver/Lamb\_Anu.zip}. Each holds exactly two files,
\texttt{Lamb\_<Scholar>.tex} and \texttt{Lamb\_<Scholar>.bib}. Every calculation step sits inside a
\texttt{worked} environment controlled by \verb|\showworkedtrue| / \verb|\showworkedfalse| in the preamble;
equations are numbered outside the worked blocks, and no sentence outside a worked block refers to a step
inside one, so switching the flag off leaves a scholar version with results, sources and definitions only.
The bibliography is \texttt{unsrt} with \texttt{bibtex}, and every entry carries a \texttt{note} saying what
was actually read.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{One rule applied throughout.} No file refers to an internal log, an audit, an earlier
error or project history, and no scholar-facing passage contains a derivation of something the scholar is
asked to check. The master carries the working; the flag-off version does not.
