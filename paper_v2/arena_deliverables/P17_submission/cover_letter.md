**Re: submission of a review manuscript — "Waves in Thermoelastic and Multiphysics Continua: Generalized Heat Conduction, Coupled Fields and Size Effects"**

Dear Editor,

I submit the manuscript named above for consideration as a review article in *Applied Mechanics Reviews*.

**1. What the manuscript is.** A review of linear wave propagation in thermoelastic and multiphysics continua, organised around one heat-flux template with three lags (Eq. (14)), from which the classical, Lord–Shulman, Green–Lindsay, Green–Naghdi, dual-phase-lag, three-phase-lag and Moore–Gibson–Thompson theories are obtained as parameter choices, with the regular and singular limits identified. It cites 414 works and treats wave problems in three dimensions, at free surfaces and interfaces, in plates and layered media, in piezoelectric and other coupled continua, and in materials with nonlocal and gradient enrichments, followed by solution methods with a benchmark, the experimental evidence base, a comparative synthesis, a pitfalls checklist and a research agenda.

**2. What is new here, compared with earlier reviews.** The field has been reviewed before, and the manuscript says so on p. 2, citing Chandrasekharaiah (1986, 1998), Hetnarski and Ignaczak (1999, 2000), Ignaczak and Ostoja-Starzewski (2010) and Tzou (2015). The differences this manuscript claims are specific and checkable:

* the reduction of the named theories to **one template**, with the reduction table (Table 4) and the singular limit τ_q → 0 at fixed τ_T identified as such (Sec. 3.2.4);
* a **dispersion polynomial** in K = k² valid for every rational heat law, derived and checked symbolically, which makes like-for-like comparison of speed, attenuation and damping possible across models that are usually tabulated separately (Eq. (34), Appendix B);
* a demonstration that the **dual-phase-lag stability threshold depends on the Taylor truncation** — τ_T = τ_q/2 for the second-order-in-τ_q variant, τ_T = τ_q for the first-order variant, τ_T = (2−√3)τ_q when both are expanded to second order — with the growth rates computed (Sec. 4.3.1, Fig. 10);
* a **quantified statement of when a truncated lag law ceases to represent the exact delay** (Sec. 4.4.2, Fig. 12);
* a **page-level provenance table** that records which attributions were checked against the printed source and what each check does not cover (Table 6) — this is, as far as I know, not a feature of the earlier reviews;
* a **five-axis taxonomy**, a seventeen-item pitfalls checklist and a decision guide (Sec. 10.1–10.2), intended so that a new model can be placed without argument and a comparison between two studies can be judged comparable or not.

These are also listed in the manuscript's Table 1, whose sixth column states where each contribution is delivered and what evidence supports it. A separate one-page statement of what is new, in the form requested for the submission file, accompanies this letter.

**3. Relation to the earlier reviews, and the balance of review and new material.** The manuscript is a review, but it does not pretend that its own synthesis is not new. Section 1 states which contributions are review (the organisation, the comparison tables, the decision guide) and which are new to the literature (the comparative dispersion polynomial and the truncation analysis). A comparison table setting this manuscript against the five earlier reviews, section by section, is being prepared for the revision; if the editor would prefer it as part of the submission file rather than the manuscript, it can be supplied either way.

**4. Length and supplementary material.** The manuscript is 157 pages, including 12 appendices (32 pages) and the reference list (28 pages). Appendices E (classified catalogue, 13 pages), J (equation verification register, 8 pages) and K (atlas of problems, 3 pages) can move to supplementary material, bringing the main text to about 100 pages with no loss of argument; a condensed key-works table would remain in the main text so that it stays self-contained, because 66 references are cited only in the catalogue appendix. I am happy to follow the editor's preference on this and on whether the two longest body sections should also be shortened.

**5. Originality and simultaneous submission.** The manuscript is original, has not been published previously in whole or in part, and is not under consideration by any other journal. No part of it is a translation or a reworking of previously published material of my own, except where the text cites my own earlier papers explicitly; the tables of Appendix K separate studies from my own group from studies from other groups, and 38 of the 414 references (9.2 %) are co-authored by me. That proportion is stated in the manuscript (Sec. 2.4).

**6. Generative-tool disclosure.** Generative artificial-intelligence tools were used in preparing this manuscript: for drafting and language editing, for writing and running the verification scripts, and for checking notation and cross-references. The manuscript discloses this in its Declarations, together with two limits that matter for a referee: not every cited work was read page by page, and the claim register records which attributions were checked against the printed source and which were not. I reviewed the content and take responsibility for it; the tools are not authors. No figure in the manuscript was generated by an image model; every figure was produced by a script from data generated by the accompanying code, or drawn from the equations as a schematic and labelled as such.

**7. Data and code availability.** The Python scripts that produce every computed figure, the fourteen benchmark cases and the parameter registers accompany the manuscript, and each figure states which script produced it. No new experimental data were generated; every measured value quoted in the manuscript is ascribed to its published source in the text.

**8. Permissions.** Eight figures are adapted from published figures and are captioned "after [reference]". Written permissions from the relevant publishers are being sought; if they are not obtained in time, those figures will be redrawn from the governing equations and the captions changed accordingly, so that the submission carries no third-party material requiring permission.

**9. Declarations.** No funding was received for this work; there is no competing interest. The corresponding author is the sole author.

I hope the manuscript is suitable for *Applied Mechanics Reviews* and I look forward to the referees' comments.
