# DOI-less references — one paragraph for the editor

Fourteen entries in the reference list carry no DOI. Six are journal articles that
either predate DOI assignment or appeared in a journal that does not register DOIs
(Born and von Kármán 1912, Duhamel 1837, Cattaneo 1948, Danilovskaya 1950, Vernotte
1958, Gulyaev 1969), one is an article in a journal that is not a Crossref member
(Kumar, Devi and Sharma 2017), one is a chapter in a multi-author book (Chadwick
1960), and four are monographs that were never registered as books (Auld 1973,
Boley and Weiner 1960, Love 1911, Scruby and Drain 1990). For two further books a
DOI exists only for a different edition: Nowacki's *Thermoelasticity* is cited in
the 1986 Pergamon edition, which is registered at chapter level under ISBN
978-0-08-024767-0 but has no book-level DOI, and Tzou's 1997 CRC edition has no DOI
at all, the registered work being the 2014 second edition. In each case the absence
of a DOI is a property of the publication rather than a gap in the reference list,
and every such entry is printed with the author, title, journal or publisher,
volume, pages and year needed to locate it.
