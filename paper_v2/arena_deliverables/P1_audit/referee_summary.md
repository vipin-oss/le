# Referee summary (skeptical read of paper_v2/main.pdf)

Source: origin/review-paper-v2 paper_v2/main.pdf (143 pages, 31 Oct 2026).
Text extracted with pypdf. Stance: Applied Mechanics Reviews / Physics Reports.
Unconfirmed items marked possible. Missing literature is tagged as general knowledge.

## Top 10 weaknesses (by severity)

1. HIGH -- Highlights / Sec. 1 (p.1, p.11). The organising claim puts Green-Lindsay (and "every linear model") through G(s) as a parameter choice. Table 5 has no GL row; Sec. 7.3 says GL "does not fit the flux template (14)"; Sec. 5 puts Guyer-Krumhansl outside the template. Quote: "through which every linear model passes".

2. HIGH -- Sec. 31 (p.83-86) with p.12. The review "does not report new experiments". Experimental evidence is a short secondary survey. For Physics Reports / AMR, a 143-page wave-dynamics review that leaves lags as fitting constants is unbalanced. The paper itself says an independently unmeasurable parameter is not a material property.

3. HIGH -- Sec. 33 Atlas (p.89-92). Fourteen problems from eleven papers, "not a representative sample", "first instalment", zero laboratory experiments, verification "thin". Table 16 is the author's own group and "not counted as independent evidence". That is not an atlas of the field.

4. MEDIUM -- Sec. 2 Table 3 (p.15-16) and Appendix D (p.98-). "Representative" streams and the classified catalogue are title-sorted and lean on the author's circle (Gupta/Barak/Kumar) plus long uncritical title lists. Sec. 34.3 admits the catalogue "does not assess the content". Several refs note "page-1 check of the author library".

5. MEDIUM -- Sec. 1 (p.12). "We use one nondimensionalisation throughout" is not borne out: figures use omega_* units, chi=tau_R=1, and front units with c_T=1 (possible across captions). The motivation (incomparable published figures) is then only partly met.

6. MEDIUM -- Sec. 8 (p.34-37). The DPL "correction" (tau_T >= tau_q/2 belongs to one Taylor truncation) is computed for coupled 1D roots and is useful, but Table 14 still says "no instability found numerically". A journal of this rank will want a theorem or a clear statement that the result is numerical. Possible: the truncation point is already in the cited Quintanilla line.

7. MEDIUM -- Title vs Sec. 24, 28 (p.68-72, p.78-80). "Multiphysics continua" and applications (tissue, geophysics, magneto, diffusion, micropolar) are mostly citation surveys. Only 1D piezo is an exact reduction. from general knowledge, not verified in this task: Biot poroelasticity and dynamic electromagnetism are not developed as wave problems.

8. MEDIUM -- Sec. 5 and history (p.14, p.25). Finite-speed heat is told as Cattaneo then LS. from general knowledge, not verified in this task: Gurtin-Pipkin hereditary conduction (1968) is absent (Pipkin does not appear in the PDF); Callaway/phonon-BTE closures beyond linear GK are not treated.

9. MEDIUM -- Structure (whole paper). 36 numbered sections, duplicate Keywords (p.2), two nomenclature tables (p.10, p.13), TED/flexure split (6.4 and 20), reflection split (12 and 14), history / taxonomy / synthesis restated three times. Length is monograph-like; repetition will try a referee.

10. MEDIUM -- Sec. 35-36 (p.94-96). Open problem 1 asks for a classification/well-posedness theorem after the Highlights claim the family is already organised. Acknowledgements: scripts "verify all reported" dispersion, damping and energy-partition results -- stronger than Sec. 34.3 (handbook values, illustrative, not for design).

## What is actually strong (not in the top 10)

Dispersion polynomial for rational G(s); GN-III has no strict finite front; net flux vs mode fractions at lossy interfaces; Zener vs Lifshitz-Roukes check; honest limitations paragraph (34.3). Those are self-contained and should be kept if the organising claims are narrowed.

## Recommendation (referee voice)

Major revision before AMR/Physics Reports: narrow the template claim to Table 5, drop GL/GK/"every linear model" from Highlights, replace the atlas with a representative validated-problem set (or delete it), and expand experimental constraints or explicitly recast the paper as a computational handbook of linear homogeneous waves -- not a review of multiphysics continua.
