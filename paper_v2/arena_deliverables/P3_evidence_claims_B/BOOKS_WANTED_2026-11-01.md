## First, the finding that matters more than any list

**No book is blocking a claim.** The register carries 111 claims at `SOURCE NOT AVAILABLE`; I audited them
against every claim-level file this project has produced, and **109 of the 111 are already covered** — by the
40-row upgrade file, the held-source pass, the book pass, the register-level pass, the sixth round, the
author's waivers, or the author-supplied works. Of the two left:

| claim | cited work | state |
|---|---|---|
| CR181 | Born and von Kármán 1912 | the 1912 note is still unheld, but Born and Huang (1954) now carries the attribution and the formula at one remove — see `BORN_HUANG_1954_READ_2026-11-01.md` |
| CR144 | Kumar, Devi and Sharma 2017 | a journal paper, not a book; papers can wait per the standing rule |

So the honest answer to "which books do you need" is: **none, to unblock anything.** Everything below is
about *strength*: the things a book would let this project do that it currently cannot do, or cannot do
independently.
