## 4. Why the batches exist at all

The `claim_check_B_batch_NNN.csv` files are not requests and not separate lists. They are the row-by-row
record of what a given source says about a given claim - 667 rows in eighteen files because the delivery
format caps a batch at 40 rows. What is added from now on is this ledger and the claim-level verdict files.
