## Job added 2026-11-01 (step 39): BK-15 executed - the twelve 12x12 moduli of Eringen & Maugin I, and 40 of 40

elastic C_KLMN, piezoelectric e_KLM, piezomagnetic q_KLM, dielectric chi^E_KL, magnetic chi^B_KL and
magnetoelectric Lambda_KL. Because there is a single potential, the 12x12 Hessian in the conjugate pairs
(E_KL, T_KL), (E_K, -P_K) and (B_K, -M_K) is symmetric by construction, and because the material is stable it is
positive definite; everything else in BK-15 follows from those two facts:

| invariant | what it tests | measured | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| I1 | the 12x12 Hessian of (5.11.1) is symmetric | max abs(W - W^T) = 0.000e+00 | PASS |
| I2 | it is positive definite (after diagonal scaling, since it spans 1e11 to 1e-8) | min eigenvalue 5.1205e-01 | PASS |
| I3 | open circuit (D = B = 0) stiffens: C* - C is PSD | min 0.000e+00, max 2.7148e+05, stiffening 1.1e-06 of max(C) | PASS |
| I4 | branches real, coupled speeds never below bare, 48 directions | min(lambda_coupled - lambda_bare) = 3.3061e+01 | PASS |
| I5 | the 6mm piezo limit reproduces C44 + e15^2/chi11 | relative error 0.000e+00 | PASS |
| I6 | coupling off returns C exactly; short circuit removes the stiffening | 0.000e+00; ratio 1e-24 | PASS |

Two numbers are quoted on purpose because they are the ones a fake would get wrong: the stiffening is **1.1e-06
of max(C)**, which is what 0.05 C/m^2 against chi = 1.2e-8 F/m actually gives (a quartz-like coupling, not a
piezoceramic), and **I2 needs diagonal scaling** - the raw double-precision spectrum of a matrix mixing 1e11 with
1e-8 is meaningless, so the check is done on D^-1/2 W D^-1/2, where positive definiteness is a congruence
invariant. `check_multifield.py` recomputes I1-I6 independently and then enforces the atlas: the six rows carry
`coverage = yes`, `applicable_benchmarks = BK-15`, and `case_level_result` stays **no** for every one of them,
because the papers themselves were still read at title and metadata level. **An executable invariant is not a
result.** The atlas therefore moves 34/0/6 to **40/0/0 of 40** - all 40 cases have an invariant, none is partial,
and 0 of 40 has a case-level result.
