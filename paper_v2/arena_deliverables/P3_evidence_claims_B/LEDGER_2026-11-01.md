The `claim_check_B_batch_NNN.csv` files are not requests and not separate lists. They are the row-by-row
record of what a given source says about a given claim - 667 rows in eighteen files because the delivery
format caps a batch at 40 rows. What is added from now on is this ledger and the claim-level verdict files.

Two things this round turned up that a reader must not lose:

1. **Cattaneo's 1948 note has two lives.** The 1948 Italian note in the Modena *Atti* (vol. 3) is the one the
   manuscript cites; the front page of the 1966 C.I.M.E. course also lists a 1958 French note, "Sur la
   conduction de la chaleur", C. R. Acad. Sci. Paris 247, p. 431 - and Tzou's bibliography prints that 1958
   note as C. R. 247, pp. 431-433. The register row is the 1948 one, and the secondary source used for CR092
   credits the 1948 date, so the two are not confused here.
2. **The word "independent" is the author's, not the sources'.** Neither Tzou nor the 2010 monograph says
   that Vernotte's note was independent of Cattaneo's; they credit two authors jointly for one model. That
   is a small wording point on CR093, not an evidence gap.
