2021 (Appl. Math. Comput.)" row. The file is *Arbitrary partitionability of product graphs* by Liu,
Nurmamat and Zhang, Appl. Math. Comput. 408 (2021) 126219 - the paper that the DOI on that row
(10.1016/j.amc.2021.126219) actually resolves to. So that row never was a second van der Merwe paper:
the register entry is a duplicate of row 71 with a DOI that belongs to an unrelated paper. It is retired
rather than counted as received, and the register row it cites (CR117) stays as it is - changing a
register verdict is a separate, evidence-bearing step.
