## How the check was done

Every file was opened with a PDF reader and its first two pages read as text: the DOI, the journal, the volume and the first page were compared with all 39 rows still on the request list, and the first author plus year were also compared as a fallback for rows that carry no DOI. Three surname+year coincidences came up and were rejected on the title:

- a 2025 *Applied Mathematical Modelling* paper by Xuhui Li et al. on functionally graded shear waves is not Wang and Li 2025 (*Fractal Fract.* 9, 794);
- a 2025 *Mechanics of Solids* paper is not Kumar, Gupta, Pathania and Barak 2025;
- a 2020 *Applied Mathematics and Mechanics* paper by Pankaj et al. on a coated piezoelectric cylinder is not Singh, Sarkar and Pal 2020 (*J. Heat Transfer* 142).

Two of the files carry no text layer at all (*a 1975 ARMA continuum theory of elastic material surfaces with its 1975 addendum*, and *surface stress in solids*); they are image-only scans, so nothing is asserted about them beyond the subject, and neither can be any of the 39 rows, none of which is a Gurtin-Murdoch surface-elasticity paper.
