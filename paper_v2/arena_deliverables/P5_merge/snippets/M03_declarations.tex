\subsection*{Data and code availability}
The scripts that produce every computed figure and every benchmark in \cref{app:repro} and

\subsection*{Use of generative tools}
Generative language-model tools were used in the preparation of this manuscript for drafting,
language editing, consistency checking of notation and cross-references, and for building the audit
tables that support the verification register in \cref{sec:eqregister}. Every technical statement,
equation, citation and numerical result was checked by the author against the cited source or against a
script in the reproducibility appendix; the tools are not authors and take no responsibility for the
content. Readers should treat the paper's claims as the author's, not as machine output.
