# P5-acquire batch 001 - acquisition summary

## Outcome

Target for this batch: 40 independent single-wave-problem papers across 7 families.
Delivered in `acquisition_list_batch_001.csv`: 8 verified records (opened-page rule satisfied).
Delivered in `acquisition_queue_unverified_batch_001.csv`: 40 additional dedup-clean candidates,
ALL UNVERIFIED (never opened; search-snippet provenance only).

FEWER THAN 40 PAPERS ARE DELIVERED IN THE VERIFIED LIST. The shortfall is caused entirely by a
tooling outage, not by a lack of candidates: a verified-candidate queue of 40 papers (enough to
reach the 40-paper target in every family) was assembled, dedup-checked and staged, but the pages
could not be opened to complete verification.

## fetch_page outage (why the batch is partial)

- The `fetch_page` tool routes every request through an Aliyun OSS proxy
  (`routify-file-proxy-sg.oss-ap-southeast-1.aliyuncs.com`). Starting partway through the
  acquisition work, EVERY fetch returned an HTTP-level `SignatureDoesNotMatch` error generated by
  the proxy itself (its own re-signing of the storage URL is invalid), for all target hosts
  (nature.com, mdpi.com, link.springer.com, doi.org, api.crossref.org, frontiersin.org,
  aimspress.com, pubs.aip.org) and all URL forms, including single-fetch turns and query-parameter
  variants.
- The outage persisted for at least 16 consecutive turns (several turns before a context
  compression boundary and roughly 12 turns after it), with no recovery at the time of delivery.
  Earlier in the session the same proxy worked normally (all 8 verified records plus several
  exploratory fetches succeeded) and recovered once after a shorter flaky stretch, so this is an
  intermittent infrastructure fault.
- Direct sandbox networking is blocked by design (`curl https://api.crossref.org/...` exits 35,
  SSL error, HTTP 000), so no bash workaround exists; `fetch_page` is the only page-opening
  channel.
- `web_search` uses a different backend and kept working. It was used to bank candidates
  (DOIs, titles, author lists, journals) and to pre-clear them against the dedup corpus, but
  search snippets do NOT satisfy the opened-page rule, so none of them entered the verified CSV.
- The opened-page rule was NOT relaxed at any point; per user decision, partial verified results
  are delivered and the rest is listed as an explicitly unverified queue.

## Unverified queue (acquisition_queue_unverified_batch_001.csv), 40 rows

Columns: acq_id, family, doi_candidate, title_candidate, notes. Every row is marked UNVERIFIED in
notes. Authors, journals and years are intentionally NOT included as fields (no guessing); where a
search snippet showed author names, that observation is preserved inside notes as provenance.
title_candidate is exactly the title string observed in the search snippet, or "-" where no title
string was captured. Every doi_candidate was grepped against the 320-DOI dedup corpus
(refs.bib + refs_extra.bib + refs_new.bib + refs_landmark_candidates.bib + atlas_targets.csv)
with 0 matches.

Queue per-family counts: F1 4, F2 7, F3 3, F4 8, F5 4, F6 6, F7 8 (= 40).

Special cases noted in the queue:
- ACQ-031 (10.1007/s11433-009-0029-x, Cao 2009, Bleustein-Gulyaev FGPM layered structure): the
  Springer landing page WAS opened during the session (title, journal, year, DOI and abstract all
  printed), but the author line was truncated ("Cao, X., Jin, F., Wang, Z. et al."), so the
  opened-page rule (full printed author list) is not yet satisfied; only the crossref author list
  is missing. This is the closest queue row to full verification.

  thermoelastic; kept as a backup candidate only.
