\section{Atlas of problems in the literature}\label{sec:atlas_ref}

This section tabulates problems drawn from the literature PDFs collected in Google Drive. Each entry is matched to its bibliography key by the DOI printed on page 1 of the PDF (matched against the {\tt doi} field in {\tt refs.bib}, {\tt refs\_extra.bib} and {\tt refs\_new.bib}). Papers whose page-1 DOI is not found in any bibliography file are listed as unmatched in {\tt atlas/MATCH\_REPORT.csv} and are not included in this table. All fields---geometry class, heat-flux model, coupled fields, boundary/interface conditions, method, main finding and evidence page---are extracted from the PDF page-1 text only; entries marked {\em --} were not available on page 1. The main finding is paraphrased from the abstract or conclusion as stated in the paper, not copied verbatim.
