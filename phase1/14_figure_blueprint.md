## 17. Figure Quality Standards

1. **Physical correctness:** All diagrams must reflect actual mathematical relationships
2. **No decorative figures:** Every figure answers a specific scientific question
3. **Editable format:** SVG, TikZ, or PGFPlots preferred over raster
4. **Source traceability:** Each figure cites its underlying references
5. **No copyrighted reproduction:** Original artwork only
