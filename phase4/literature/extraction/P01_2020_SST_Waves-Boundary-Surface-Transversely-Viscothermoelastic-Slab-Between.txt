1 Introduction
By nature, in the field of geosciences, civil engineering, and seismology having continuous changes in
their properties which is the cause of propagation of the elastic wave. Through the earth (seismic
waves) the elastic wave has to travel through various interfaces and layers. These waves are affected
by the properties of the layer through which they travel and having different velocities. The signals
obtained by these waves help provide information about the exploration of valuable materials such as
crystals, minerals, and metals, etc, and the internal structures of the earth. This is one of the best
techniques in terms of time and cost factors.
As the importance of anisotropic devices has increased in many fields of optics and
microwaves, wave propagation in anisotropic media has been widely studied over in the last decades.
The anisotropic nature stems from the polarization or magnetization that can occur in materials when
external fields pass by. Along with the free boundary of an elastic half-space plain wave propagation
mathematical modeling has been a subject of sustained interest for many years. Keith and Crampin [2]
derived a formula for calculating energy splitting between waves arising from an incident of plane

Kumar, Sharma, and Ram [4], Kumar and Mukhopadhyay [5], Lee and Lee [6], Othman [7], Kaushal,

Warren and Chen [17] exploration the propagation of the wave in thermoelasticity theory of
two – temperatures. Green and Naghdi [18], reconstruct the thermoelasticity theories and define three
models G N -I, II, and III. In the linearized version, the classical thermoelastic model (based on
Fourier's law) matches the model-I and at a finite speed thermal wave propagation permitted in model-
II and model-III linearized version. G N -II model, initialization with incorporating the thermal
displacement gradient amongst other constitutive variables and reduced energy equation derived the
constitutive equations. This model retains the dissipation of thermal energy that not exists in other
established thermoelastic models [1]. G N -III model, initialization with a temperature gradient,
thermal displacement gradient amongst the constitutive variables, and reduced energy equation
derived the constitutive equations. Green and Naghdi [19], a theory of linearized version for isotropic
and homogeneous materials in terms of the temperature and displacement fields involves the
extraction of a complete set of governing equations and for relating initial-boundary value problem a
proof of solution uniqueness. In the theory of thermoelasticity, with and without thermoelasticity and
energy dissipation with two temperatures. comprehensive work has been done. Youssef [20] derived a
new generalized thermoelasticity theory without energy dissipation in the isotropic and homogeneous
body by keeping into mind two-temperature generalized thermoelasticity theory. The following
researchers Youssef [21], Kumar, Chawla, and Abbas [22], Sharma, and Kumar [23], Sharma and
Marin [24], and Sharma and Bhargav [25] have studied a large number of problems containing two
temperatures.
Like the Sun, Earth and other planets are most large bodies having different angular velocities
respectively. The Earth also has magnetical property so it is necessary to magnetic field effect taking
into account in rotating medium during the study of thermoelastic wave propagation. Several
researchers trying to study in an infinite elastic medium rotating with angular velocity the propagation
of finite thermoelastic waves. Atwa and Jahangir [26], Das and Kanoria [27] have observed the effect
of rotation in generalized thermoelasticity in various problems.
There are a lot of applications of Sandwich structures such as automobiles, aircraft, ships, and
boats due to impact resistance and bending strength at a lightweight. The dynamic applications
inspired dynamic flexural deformation of multilayer plates and beams and different studies of wave

propagation. Elphinstone and Lakhtakia [28] have examined the reaction of the plane wave incident
on the sandwich’s chiral solid slab between two elastic half-spaces. Khurana and Tomar [29]
discussed longitudinal wave response of chiral slab interposed between micropolar elastic solid half-
spaces. Wave propagation in the sandwich layer has been investigated by many researchers
Deshpande and Fleck [30], Liu and Bhattacharya [31], Chaudhary, Kaushik, and Tomar [32].
In viscoelasticity, different types of problems are discussed by different authors. Freudenthal
[33] pointed out the viscous effects of most solids when subjected to dynamic loading. One of the
macroscopic mechanical models, Kelvin –Voigt model where the deformation is not time-independent
represents the delayed elastic response subjected to stress. To describe the viscoelastic behavior of
material this model is often used. In the theory of thermoviscoelasticity, Yan and Scalia [34] studied
some theorems. Discontinuity of integral type wave through a linear thermoviscoelastic solid
investigated by Borrelli and Patria [35]. The viscoelastic half-space problem of torsional body forces
studied by Pal [36]. For viscoelastic materials, non-linear generalized Maxwell fluid was investigated
by Corr et al. [37]. Kumar, Chawla, and Abbas [38], studied propagation of the wave in three phase-
lag models of anisotropic thermoelastic medium with the effect of viscosity. The effect of periodic
loading, rotation, and magnetic field on radial vibrations non-homogeneous thermo-viscoelastic was
investigated by AI-Basyouni, Mahmoud, and Alzahrani [39]. With temperature-dependent material
properties, coupled one – dimensional viscoelastic and thermal waves in media analyzed by Hilton
[40]. Yadav, Kalkal, and Deswal [41] investigated a state-space problem of Two-Temperature
generalized Thermo viscoelasticity with fractional order strain subjected to moving heat source.

is made incident upon transversely isotropic magneto Viscothermoelastic solid with an appropriate set
of boundary conditions. We have presented the transmission and reflection coefficients obtained
separately by using MATLAB and above said boundary conditions. The graphical behaviors are
