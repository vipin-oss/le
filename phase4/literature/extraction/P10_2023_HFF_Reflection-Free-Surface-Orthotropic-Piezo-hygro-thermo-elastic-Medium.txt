1.Introduction
Mostmodernengineeringmaterials,suchasbiotissues,concrete,compositesandfoams,are
heatsensitive(thermoscopic)andhygroscopicbecauseofthematerial’sporosity,crackand
atomic structure. Smart materials, especially piezoelectric materials, can combine
mechanical,electricalandhygro-thermalpropertiestoimprovetheperformanceofnetworks
throughactivestiffening,activedampingandreducingthesizeofvibrations(Smith,2005).
Heatandmoisturecannotbedisregardedwhenaddressingthemechanicsofthesematerials
andstructures(Sihetal.,1986;Hens,2012).“Hygro-thermaleffect”referstotheinteraction
betweentemperatureandmoisture.Inlightofthis,thatiscrucialtocontinueresearchingthe
piezoelectriccompositematerialsbehaveinahygro-thermalenvironment.
Piezoelectric materials have garnered much attention in the domains of actuation,
sensing,intelligentstructuresandmechanicalsystems.Theterminology“piezoelectricity”
describes a crystal’s capacity to produce a voltage when stressed mechanically.
PiezoelectricitywasinitiallydiscoveredbytheCuriebrothers(CurieandCurie,1880).The
governingequationforcouplingbetweenthetemperatureandpiezoelectricmaterial(piezo-
thermo-elastic material)hasbeendevelopedbyMindlin(1974).Thefundamentalphysical
laws for piezo-thermo-elastic materials have been investigated by Nowacki (1978, 1979).
Researchersandengineersputalotofeffortintoperfectpiezoelectricmaterialsinthepast
few decades and made their commercial use a reality. Lewis and his team investigated
different heat and fluid flow issue models, as described in references (Lewis and Garner,
1972;Lewisetal.,1991,1997,2004).Marinetal.(2013)conductedastudyonthetheoryof
thermoelasticity,whichincorporatesheatconductionindeformablebodiesandaccountsfor
the influence of two temperatures. Alzahraniet al. (2020) used the eigenvalues methodto
investigateatwo-dimensionalporousmaterialwithvaryingconductivity,includingweak,
strongandnormalconductivity.Numerousresearchershaveinvestigatedvariousproblems
relatedtothereflectionandtransmissionofplanewaves(SharmaandMarin,2013;Marin
etal.,2017;Yadav,2020,2022a;Pathaniaetal.,2022;Yadav,2022b;Pathaniaetal.,2023).
Raja et al. (2004b), using the virtual work concept, demonstrate the impact of active
stiffness on the dynamic behavior of piezo-hygro-thermo-elastic (PHTE) laminates and a
coupledpiezoelectricfiniteelementformulationwithahygro-thermalstrainfieldhasbeen
