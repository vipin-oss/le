A classical-two-temperature (CTT) model of heat conduction in deformable bodies was
developedbyChenandGurtin(1968),ChenandWilliams(1968),andChenetal.(1969).In
this theory, the heat conduction equation relies on thermal and conductive temperature
HFF fluctuations.Youssef(2006)formulatedtheCTTmodelanduniquenesstheoremsaboutthe
33,2 generalizedmodelofthermoelasticitybasedontheLSmodel.YoussefandEl-Bary(2018)
replaced the CTT model with the hyperbolic-two-temperature (HTT) model. They
eliminatedtheconundrumofinfinitespeedpropagationofthermalwavesbyincorporating
theaccelerationofthethermalandconductivetemperatures.Sheoranetal.(2020)studiedthe
wave propagation in an initially stressed rotating thermo-diffusive medium with CTT.
914 BassiounyandRajagopalan(2020)investigatedthethermoelasticmodelexposedtothermal
stressintheframeworkofHTTtheoryusingfractionalderivatives.
The nonlocal theory of thermoelasticity assumes that the different physical quantities
suchas(bodyforce,mass,internalenergyandentropy)areinfluencednotonlybythevalues
ofindependentconstitutivevariablesbutalsoatagivenpointbythevaluesforthewhole
system. The traditional continuum elasticity theory fails to characterize the constitutive
relationshipforthesematerials.Tosolvetheshortcomingsoftheconventional(local)theory
of thermoelasticity, Edelen and Laws (1971), Edelen et al. (1971) and Eringen and Edelen
(1972)createdthenonlocaltheoryofthermoelasticity.Polizzotto(2001),Chakraborty(2007)
andSinghetal.(2017)expandedtheideaofnonlocalitytoelasticityandavarietyofother
domains.Severalresearchers,includingVashishth andSukhija (2015), Barak etal.(2017),
Kumaretal.(2018,2019,2021)investigatedtheenergysharesofreflectedandtransmitted
waves at the interface of different types of mediums. Recently, Kumar et al. (2021)
investigatedthepropagationcharacteristics(attenuation,velocity,lociofverticalslowness,
phase shift, energy) of plane harmonic waves on the stress-free surface of a partially
saturated poro-thermoelastic media. Later, Kumar et al. (2022) examined the partition of
incidentenergyamongdistinctreflectedandrefractedwavesattheweldedinterfaceofan
elasticsolidandunsaturatedporo-thermoelasticsolid.
In recent years, piezoelectric materials have grown in popularity for dynamic sensing,
actuationandactivestructuralandmechanicalsystemscontrol.KumarandSharma(2017)
discussed the reflection of plane waves at the interface of elastic/piezothermoelastic solid
half-spaceswithfractional-orderderivatives.Thenonlocalgeneralizedelasticitytheoryfor
piezothermoelastic materials with thermal characteristics was established by Li and He
(2018). In the context of MDD and two-temperature theories, Sur and Mondal (2020)
developedanonlocalmathematicalmodel.YangandChen(2020)investigatedthenonlocal
DPL heat theory for nonlocal thermal–viscoelastic materials. Mondal and Othman (2021)
studiedtheeffectofmemoryonpiezothermoelasticmediumunderthreedifferenttheories.
KumarandSharma(2021)usedtheCTTtheorytoinvestigatetheresponseofenergyratios
attheelasticandpiezothermoelasticinterfaces.
According to the literature mentioned above, there is no substantial research on the
considered model incorporating fluid half-space (FHS) and nonlocal orthotropic
piezothermoelastichalf-space(NOPHS)withhyperbolictwo-temperatureDPLandmemory.
