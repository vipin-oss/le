Abstract
Purpose–ThispaperaddressesasignificantresearchgapinthestudyofRayleighsurfacewavepropagation
within a piezoelectric medium characterized by piezoelectric properties, thermal effects and voids. Previous
researchhasoftenoverlookedthecrucialaspectsrelatedtovoids.Thisstudyaimstoprovideanalyticalsolutions
forRayleighwavespropagatingthroughamediumconsistingofanonlocalpiezo-thermo-elasticmaterialwith
voidsundertheMoore–Gibson–Thompsonthermo-elasticitytheorywithmemorydependencies.
Design/methodology/approach–Theanalyticalsolutionsarederivedusingawave-modemethod,and
rootsarecomputedfromthecharacteristicequationusingtheDurand–Kernermethod.Theserootsarethen
filteredbasedonthedecayconditionofsurfacewaves.Theanalysispertainstoamediumsubjectedtostress-
freeandisothermalboundaryconditions.
Findings– Computationalsimulationsareperformedtodeterminetheattenuationcoefficientandphase
velocityofRayleighwaves.Thisinvestigationgoesbeyondmerecalculationsandexaminesparticlemotion
togaindeeperinsightsintoRayleighwavepropagation.Furthermore,thisinvestigateshowkernelfunction
andnonlocalparametersinfluencethesewavephenomena.
Research limitations/implications – The results of this study reveal several unique cases that
significantlycontributetotheunderstandingofRayleighwavepropagationwithinthisintricatematerial
system,particularlyinthepresenceofvoids.
Practical implications – This investigation provides valuable insights into the synergistic dynamics
amongpiezoelectricconstituents,voidstructuresandRayleighwavepropagation,enablingadvancementsin
sensortechnology,augmentedenergyharvestingmethodologiesandpioneeringseismicmonitoringapproaches.
Originality/value–Thisstudyformulatesanovelgoverningequationforanonlocalpiezo-thermo-elastic

HFF 1. Introduction
Surfacewavespropagatealongtheboundaryofthemediumandcontaininformationabout
surface imperfections. Surface waves are commonly classified as Love, Stoneley and
Rayleigh waves. Rayleigh (1885), in 1885, first explained the waves as moving along an
elasticsolid’splanesurface.PressandHealy(1957)calculatedtheRayleighwaveabsorption
coefficient in low-loss media. Rayleigh wave coefficients at vertical boundaries when
movingfromonewaveguidetypetoanotherwerecalculatedbyMcGarrandAlsop(1967).
Inscientificexploration,thepioneeringduoofCurieandCurie(1880),whileinvestigating
the pyroelectric attributes of specific crystal structures, stumbled upon a remarkable
phenomenon known as piezoelectricity. Mindlin (1974) laid the foundation by formulating
governing equations that elucidate the intricate relationship between temperature and
piezoelectricmaterials.Expandinguponthisgroundwork,Nowacki(1978,1979)developed
thecoupledthermoelasticdiffusionequation,advancingourcomprehensionoftheinterplay
between these fundamental properties. Inspired by Mindlin’s pioneering work,
Chandrasekharaiah(1988)embarkedondeeperexploration,introducingthegroundbreaking
conceptoffinitewavepropagationspeedintothermo-piezoelectricity.Lewisetal.(1991,1997,
2004) conducted an in-depth treatment of various heat conduction problems. Barak et al.
(2023)haveembarkedonafocusedresearchjourneyspecificallycenteredaroundtheanalysis
ofenergydistributionwithinvariouspiezo-thermo-elasticinterfacemodels.
Incorporating voids within these piezoelectric thermoelastic materials introduces an
added layer of complexity and versatility. These voids, often nanoengineered to precise
specifications, influence the material’s thermal and mechanical behavior, enhancing its
overall performance. Cowin and Nunziato (1983) have investigated linear and nonlinear
theories of elastic material with voids. Based on the Cowin–Nunziato model, Iovane and
Nasedkin (2010a) developed the linear theory of piezoelectric materials with voids for
porosity change properties. Significant advancements in nonlocal interaction theory have
been attributed to the pioneering work of Edelen and Laws (1971). Building upon these
foundations and using the principles of global balance laws and thermodynamics law,
Eringen and Edelen (1972) expanded the nonlocal theory, providing a comprehensive
framework for comprehending nonlocal effects withinelastic materials. In recent years, a
notable surge in scientific investigations has centered around the Moore–Gibson–
Thompson(MGT)equation(ThompsonandBeavers,1972;Marchandetal.,2012;Lasiecka
andWang,2015;PellicerandSol(cid:1)a-Morales,2019).Developedfromadifferentialequationof
third order, this equation has played a pivotal role in unraveling various aspects of fluid
dynamics.Notably,Quintanilla(2019)hasachievedsignificantprogressbyintroducingan
innovativethermoelasticheattransfermodelderivedfromtheMGTequation.
Theinceptionofthememory-dependentderivative(MDD)conceptcanbeattributed
to the groundbreaking work of Wang and Li (2011). This concept delves into the
elucidationoftheconversionoftheCaputoandMainarditypefractionalderivativeinto
anintegralrepresentation,encompassingtheintegrationofakernelfunctionK(t(cid:1)z).
