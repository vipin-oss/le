the FOD
The thermal time delay factor plays a critical role in various
practical applications of generalized thermoelasticity. For
example, in the design of thermal insulation materials,
understanding the behavior of heat transfer under different
conditions is essential for achieving the desired performance.
The thermal time delay factor can help capture the effects of
heat storage and transport within the material, which are
crucial considerations in such applications. Similarly, in the
development of thermal energy storage systems, accurate
modeling of the thermal behavior of materials over time is
essential to ensure efficient and reliable performance.

9. Conclusions
The present study investigates the behavior of initially
stressed transversely isotropic piezothermoelastic material
under the influence of memory-dependent and fractional-
order derivatives. Specifically, we compare the effects of
these parameters on the various distribution profiles using
the triple-phase lag model. One of the main innovations of
our research is the integration of memory-dependent deriva-
tive and fractional-order derivative into the coupled govern-
ing equations of the model. Analytical solutions for
piezoethermoelastic medium have been developed and
