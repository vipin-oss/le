1.Introduction
Semiconductors play a crucial role in regulating electron flow and form the foundation of
modern electronics. Piezo-thermo-electric semiconductors combine the unique properties
ofthermoelectricity,piezoelectricityandsemiconductors.Incorporatingtheamalgamationof
thermoelectricity,piezoelectricityandsemiconductorproperties,piezo-thermo-semiconductors
emerge as a groundbreaking solution for harvesting thermal and mechanical energy to
generate electricity. This innovative technique presents a wide range of opportunities for
effectiveenergyharvesting,enablingtheconversionofhithertountappedpowerfromvarious
sources, such as human motion, mechanical vibrations or temperature differentials, into
valuableelectricalenergy.Piezoelectricity,aphenomenoninwhichmechanicalstresscauses

uponthisconcept,Mindlin(1974)developedtheideaofpiezoelectricitywiththermaleffectand
presentedthefundamentalequationsforpiezothermoelasticplates.Subsequently,Nowacki
(1978) conducted further investigations into the fundamental principles that govern piezo
thermoelasticmaterials.

10.Futurescope
Futureresearchmightexpandonthefindingsofthisstudybyinvestigatingmoreintricate
variantsandusesoftheuniquegeneralizedphoto-piezo-thermo-elasticsemiconductor.For
instance, increasing the scope of the study to include nonhomogeneous or anisotropic
semiconductor materials can help us better understand how they behave in various
scenarios. The performance and robustness of the semiconductor in real-world scenarios
may also be revealed by examining how it reacts to dynamic external stimuli like
mechanical vibrations or external pressures. In addition, to optimize the semiconductor’s
design for particular applications, the effects of various material qualities and boundary
conditionsonthebehaviorofthesemiconductormightbeinvestigated.Thebehaviourofthe
semiconductorincomplexsituationsmayalsobebetterunderstoodbytakingintoaccount
nonlineareffectsorconnectionswithotherphysicalprocesses.
