Abstract
Thispaperreportsthebehaviorofplanewavepropagationthroughtheinterfaceofanelastic
half-space (ES) and a transversely isotropic piezoviscothermoelastic half-space composed
ofdualphaselagandhyperbolictwo-temperature(PTHD).Twowavesarereflectedwhen
Pwavesmovelongitudinally,orSVwavesmovetransversallytoreachtheESmedium,and
thefourwavesaretransmittedthroughthePTHDmedium.Theamplituderatiosforreflected
and transmitted waves are determined bysatisfying the boundary conditions.These ratios
aresubsequentlyutilizedtocalculateenergyratiosforthosewaves.Theeffectsofviscosity,
hyperbolictwo-temperature(HTT),classicaltwo-temperature(CTT),andone-temperature
(OTT)ontheenergyratiosareanalyzed.Thebalanceofenergyconservationisanalyzedfor
somecases.

Biot’s classical thermoelastic theory, introduced in (Biot 1956), relies on a parabolic

Thedevelopmentofthegoverningequationforpiezo-thermoelasticmaterialscanbetraced

reasonabletoconsiderthecontributionofheatconductiontoentropyusingonetemperature

turemeasurementsinvolved.Theconductiveandthermodynamictemperaturesweretaken

atures.(Sharma2013)exploredthewavephenomenonatthemicropolarelastichalf-space

temperature(HTT)theorybymodifyingtheexistinggeneralizedthermoelasticapproach.
Viscous materials, such as amorphous, semi-crystalline, polymers, and bio-polymers,
havesignificantapplicationsinvariousengineeringfields,includingcivil,geotechnical,and
biomechanical. The Voigt model (Voigt 1887) is a popular mechanical model to demon-
stratetheviscoelasticbehaviorofmaterials.Thismodelelucidatestheresponseofanelas-

recoverable. (Chatterjee et al. 2014; Dhua et al. 2022; Nath and Dhua 2022) explored di-
versereflectionproblemsinanisotropicmedia,examiningvariousparameterssuchasinitial
stress,viscosity,andthereinforcingmedium.Numerouscomprehensivestudieshavedelved
intoarangeofissueswithinthedomainsofviscoelasticityandthermo-viscoelasticity.These
investigations,coupledwithsignificantextensions,areexemplifiedbytheworksof(Abouel-
regalandMondal2022;Awwadetal.2022;El-Dhabaetal.2023;KhalilandAbouelregal
2022; Zenkour and Abouelregal 2022). (Barak et al. 2023c,a; Gupta and Barak 2023a,b;
Yadav et al. 2023) conducted in-depth investigations into diverse reflection and deforma-
tion problems. Their analyses included the examination of influential parameters such as
fractionalorderderivatives,moisturecontent,stiffness,andmemory-dependentderivatives.
