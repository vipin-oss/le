Abstract.Thisstudyintroducesatwo-dimensionalthermoelasticmodelforahomogeneousisotropichalf-spacewith
double porosity underlying an inviscid liquid half-space featuring temperature variations. The model incorporates

The investigation highlights dispersion, attenuation, and other effects affected by the thermal properties and the
presenceofvoids.Usingplanewavesolutionsandboundaryconditionsattheinterface,aconciseexpressionforthe
frequencyequationofthemodelhasbeenderived. Furthermore,themagnitudesofthedisplacementsinthesolid

preciselydetermined. Inthegraphicalsection,computer-simulatedresultsofvariouswaveprofilesformagnesium
crystalmaterialhavebeengeneratedfordifferentheatconductionthermoelasticmodels. Thestudy’simplications
spanvariousfields,suchashydrology,engineering,ultrasonics,navigation,andelectronics.
