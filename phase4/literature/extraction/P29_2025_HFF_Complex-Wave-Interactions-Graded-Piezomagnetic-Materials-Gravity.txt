1. Introduction
The study of wave phenomena is indispensable in fields such as seismic exploration,
earthquakeengineeringandgeophysics.Wavesserveasinvaluabletoolsforunderstanding
the properties of the medium through which they propagate. Previous research has
predominantly focused on wave dynamics in macro-scale media and composite layered
structures, as explored by Guha and Singh (2022b); Singh and Guha (2022); Singh et al.
(2022); Guha and Singh (2022a); Guha and Singh (2023); Singh and Guha (2023); Guha
etal.(2023);Pathaniaetal.(2024);BiswasandGuha(2024);Xieetal.(2024);ARetal.
(2024); Dutta et al. (2024); Das et al. (2025). Some relevant studies involving different
thermaltheorieswerealsoconductedbyseveralresearchers(Carreraetal.,2015;Alzahrani
andAbbas,2020;Zhengetal.,2024;Hanetal.,2024).
The rapid advancement of smaller and more efficient technologies has placed micro-
electromechanical systems (MEMS) at the forefront of nanotechnology development (Liu
etal.,2025;Wangetal.,2023b).ManyMEMSareconstructedfrompiezoelectric/magnetic
materials, where strain-induced electric/magnetic fields are fundamental to their operation
(Guoand Alam, 2025; Zhao et al., 2025b). Insuch systems, non-uniform strainresults in
straingradients,whichsignificantly influence themechanical behaviorofpiezoelectricsat
themicroscale–raisingquestionsaboutthevalidityofclassicalelasticitytheoriesdeveloped
for macroscale applications (Zhang et al., 2025; Alam et al., 2025). To address this,
researchers have proposed a new electro-mechanical effect – flexoelectricity (Wei et al.,
2021;Quetal.,2023)–anemergingareaofstudyinnano/microtechnology.Recentfindings
suggestthatflexoelectricityprovidesamoreaccurateexplanationofmicroscalebehaviorin
piezo materials, offering new possibilities for innovative applications in nano and
microtechnologies.Flexoelectricityisthephenomenonofgeneratingelectricalpolarization
inresponsetonon-uniformstrains.Unlikeconventionalpiezoelectricity,whicharisesfrom
crystallographicsymmetry,flexoelectricitycaninducepolarizationinanycrystal,regardless
ofitssymmetry.Asauniversalphenomenonwithgreaterversatility,flexoelectricitypresents
a promising alternative or complement to traditional piezoelectrics, particularly for
nanoscaleapplications(Singhaletal.,2024).
Gravity is essential in influencing natural processes, impacting everything from wave
movementtomaterialstabilityunderdifferentforces.Itseffectsarepivotalinphysicsand
engineering, shaping structural responses, wave patterns and stress distribution in various
systems. Bromwich (1898) explored gravity impact on elastic wave behavior, particularly
within vibrating elastic spheres, establishing a basis for understanding wave dynamics in
gravitational fields. Extending this line of inquiry, Abd-Alla and Ahmed (2003) analyzed
StoneleyandRayleighwavesinanon-uniformorthotropicmaterialundertheinfluenceof
gravity. Othman and Lotfy (2013) introduced the effects of magnetic fields and rotational
forces on fiber-reinforced thermoelastic materials through advanced thermal theories,
comparing outcomes acrossmultiple theoretical models undergravity. Elhag et al. (2022)
analyzed plane wave reflection from an electro-magneto-thermo-microstretch medium
