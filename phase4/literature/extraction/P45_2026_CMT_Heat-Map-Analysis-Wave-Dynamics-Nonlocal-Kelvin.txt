Abstract Thisworkinvestigatesthecoupledthermo–electro–mechanicalbehaviorofapiezo-semiconductor
medium by integrating spatial nonlocality, temporal nonlocality, Kelvin–Voigt (KV) viscoelasticity, and
memory-dependent derivatives into a unified analytical framework. The governing equations combine a
Klein–Gordon (KG) type nonlocal operator with three-phase-lag heat conduction, semiconductor transport,
andpiezoelectriccoupling,andaresolvedusingthenormal-modeapproach.Theanalysisrevealsthatwave-
fieldmodificationsremainhighlyconcentratedwithinathinboundary-interactionzonedeterminedbytheKG
nonlocalparameters,wherethecompetingeffectsofspatialnonlocality,temporalrelaxation,KVviscosity,and
memory-drivenkernelsjointlyinfluencethepenetrationandstrengthofthermo–electro–mechanicalresponses.
Spatial nonlocality suppresses the mechanical deformation and reduces the near-surface thermal amplitude,
while simultaneously intensifying the coupled stress, carrier concentration, electric potential, and electric-
displacementfields.Temporalrelaxationmechanismsmoderatethesevariationsbyredistributingamplitudes
overdepthandsmoothingsharpspatialgradients.KV-viscositysignificantlysuppressestheelectricalfields.
By capturing the coupled roles of nonlocality, viscoelasticity, thermal relaxation, and memory-driven carri-
er–electromechanicalinteractions,themodelprovidesacomprehensivebasisforanalyzingmultiphysicswave
behaviorinpiezoelectricsemiconductormediarelevanttoSAWsensors,acousto-electroniccomponents,trans-
ducers,andhigh-frequencysignal-processingdevices.

1 Introduction
Viscoelasticmaterialsareakeyclassofsolidsthatcombineelasticenergystoragewithtime-dependentviscous
dissipation.Incontemporaryelectronicandenergy-conversionsystems,theseeffectsarefurtheramplifiedby
couplingwithelectric,thermal,andcarrier-transportprocesses.Semiconductors,withtheirtunableelectronic
properties,canexhibitpiezoelectricorflexoelectricbehavior,enderingtheelectromechanicalcouplingintrin-
sicallyfrequency-dependentandsensitivetoviscoelasticeffects,evenwithinalinearconstitutiveframework
[1–3].Thiscouplingenablesmultifunctionaldevicesinwhichmechanicaldeformation,thermalgradients,and
electricfieldsaretightlyinterconnectedandmutuallyinfluential.
Mathematical modelling of such coupled phenomena originated in early work on elastic wave propaga-
tion. Classical thermoelastic theories were constrained by the unrealistic assumption that mechanical and
thermaldisturbancespropagateinstantaneously.Biot’s[4]coupledthermoelastictheoryaddressedthisissue
by incorporatingFourier heat conduction and establishing a thermodynamically consistent relation between
temperatureanddeformationfields.Laterdevelopments[5–7]introducedrelaxationtimesintothegoverning
equations,eliminatingtheparadoxofinfinitesignalspeedandleadingtothegeneralizedthermoelasticmodels
now widely employed. At the microscale, Tzou [8] introduced the dual-phase-lag conduction law, and later
the three-phase-lag model [9] was proposed by the Roy Choudhuri to better represent phonon-based relax-
ation mechanisms. These developments significantly broadened the applicability of thermoelastic theory to
microstructuredandthermallysensitivematerials.
Paralleltotheseadvances,nonlocalelasticityemergedasapowerfulframeworkforcapturinglong-range
interactionsatsmallscales.BeginningwiththeearlyconceptsofEdelenandLaws[10],Eringen[11]established
a differential nonlocal formulation grounded in thermodynamic constraints. Although extensively used, this
model has faced criticism for its inconsistencies in certain boundary-value settings, as shown by Romano,
Kaplunov, and others [12, 13]. More recent efforts, such as the weakly nonlocal formulations by Anh and

Vinh [14], and the Klein–Gordon-type nonlocal models developed by Lazar and Agiasofitou [15], provide
improvedmathematicalstructurebyincorporatingbothcharacteristiclengthandintrinsictimescalesintothe
constitutiverelations.
Interestinpiezoelectricsemiconductorshasgrownrapidlybecausetheyofferanaturalplatformtointe-
gratemechanicaldeformation,electricfields,thermalgradients,andcarriertransport[16].Mechanicalloading
produces polarization charges, thermal effects induce additional electric potentials, and semiconductor car-
riersresponddynamicallytoallthesefields.Mindlin’s[17]earlyformulationestablishedthefoundationfor
thermo-piezoelectric coupling, which was later expanded by Chandrasekharaiah [18] through finite-speed
thermal propagation models. Aouadi [19] further generalized the theory by allowing thermally dependent
materialcoefficients.Lotfyandcollaborators[31–33]haveenrichedthefieldthroughextensiveinvestigations
of magneto-thermo-elastic and microtemperature dynamics in semiconductor media under various loading
conditions,includingrampheating,Hall-currenteffects,andphotothermalexcitation.

9 Conclusions
Thisstudydevelopsacoupledmulti-physicsframeworktoexaminethethermo–electro–mechanicalresponse
ofapiezo-semiconductormediumbyincorporatingspatialandtemporalnonlocality,Kelvin–Voigtviscoelas-
ticity,andmemory-dependentderivativeeffectswithinaunifiedanalyticalformulation.Thenumericalresults
illustratedinFigs.2,3,4,5and6provideinsightintothedepth-dependentbehavioroftheinteractingfield
variablesundertheadoptedmaterialparametersandboundaryconditions.Theprincipalobservationsmaybe
summarizedasfollows.
• The inclusion of viscosity modifies the wave response by increasing the magnitude of displacement and
enhancing the carrier concentration and electric potential fields, while reducing the penetration depth of
electric displacement. Its influence on temperature and mechanical stress varies with depth, indicating a
position-dependentredistributionofthermo-mechanicalenergy.
• Amongtheconsideredheat-conductionmodels,thethree-phase-lag(TPL)formulationyieldscomparatively
higherpeaksintheelectricpotentialandelectricdisplacementdistributionsunderthechosenparameterset.
Incontrast,thedisplacementresponseremainsrelativelylowerthanthatpredictedbytheBiot,LS,andDPL
models,whilestress,carrierconcentration,andtemperatureprofilesexhibitdistinctdepth-wisevariations
acrossthemodels.

• Spatialnonlocalityintroducespronounceddeviationsprimarilywithinathinnear-surfaceinteractionregion.
Beyondthisnarrowlayer,thefieldresponsesrapidlyconvergetowardthepredictionsofthecorresponding
localmodel,indicatingafiniterangeofnonlocalinteraction.
• Temporal nonlocality produces comparatively broader modifications than spatial nonlocality. For small
relaxationtimes,itseffectremainsconfinednearthesurface;however,withincreasingtemporalmemory,
thedeviationsextenddeeperintothemedium,reshapingtheoverallfielddistributionratherthanremaining
boundary-localized.
• Variations in the memory-dependent derivative (MDD) kernel parameter influence the response mainly
within the surface zone. Displacement shows amplification, whereas carrier concentration, temperature,
electricpotential,andelectricdisplacementtendtoattenuate.Thestressfieldexhibitsamixedtrend,with
enhancementnearthesurfaceandreductionintheinterior,whiledeeperregionsremainrelativelyinsensitive
tokernelvariations.
• The spatial distributions of all coupled fields remain strongly depth-dependent. Mechanical and thermal
responsesareconcentratedneartheactivesurfaceregionanddecayrapidlywithdistance,whereaselectrical
fieldsextendmorebroadlyintothemedium,withelectricdisplacementattainingitspeakwithintheinterior
domain.
Overall,thepresentedformulationoffersatheoreticalbasisforunderstandingcoupledwaveinteractionsin
piezo-semiconductormaterialswheresurface-dominatedthermo-mechanicaleffectsinteractwithelectrically
governedinteriorresponses.Suchmultiphysicscouplingmayberelevantintheanalysisofwave-basedsensing
structures,semiconductoracousticdevices,andthermallysensitivemicroelectroniclayers.
10 Limitationsofthestudy
The present investigation is developed within an analytical and linear theoretical framework. The material
isassumedtobehomogeneousandorthotropic,andtheboundaryconditionsareidealizedinnature.Effects
suchasgeometricnonlinearity,materialinhomogeneity,interfacialimperfections,andlarge-amplitudewave
interactions are not considered in the current formulation. Furthermore, the numerical simulations are per-
formedforaselectedsetofmaterialconstants(CdSe)andparameterranges,andtheresultsmayvaryforother
semiconductororpiezoelectricmaterials.Experimentalvalidationoftheproposedmodelalsoremainsanopen
directionforfutureresearch.
