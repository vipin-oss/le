\section{Figure Specifications}
\label{app:figures}

This appendix lists every figure in the manuscript, together with the section in which it appears, the scientific claim it is used to support, and the verification status of the content it depicts. All eighteen figures are original vector graphics prepared for this review; none reproduces the artwork of a published paper. The stated validation refers to the scientific content of the figure --- the equations, wave speeds, conservation identities and constitutive relations drawn on it --- and not merely to the rendering.

\subsection{Figure format, provenance and location}

All eighteen figures are authored as SVG vector graphics with live text, so that every label remains editable and searchable:

\begin{itemize}
\item Source files: \texttt{phase4/manuscript/figures/source/figXX\_name.svg} (the compiled set)
\item Earlier working set: \texttt{phase4/figures/source/figXX\_name.svg} (retained for provenance; superseded by the compiled set)
\item Rendered copies: produced at build time as PDF by the conversion step in the continuous-integration workflow, and placed in the same directory before the manuscript is typeset
\end{itemize}

The SVG format guarantees scalability without quality loss, editable text and mathematical notation, consistent typography across all figures, and colour schemes that remain legible in greyscale. Figure titles are carried in the manuscript captions rather than inside the artwork, so that the numbering is generated by \LaTeX{} and cannot drift out of step with the text.

\subsection{Figures requiring full-text verification}

No figure depends on unpublished data, on numerical output that is not reproducible from the stated equations, or on the artwork of any published paper. Every relationship depicted is drawn from a constitutive relation, a balance law or a wave-speed formula that is stated and cited in the corresponding section of the manuscript, and the conservation identity shown in F-13 and F-14 is checked algebraically in the balance derivations of Appendix~B.
