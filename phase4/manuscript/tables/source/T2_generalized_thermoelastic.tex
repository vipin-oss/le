Dual-phase-lag (DPL) & Two phase lags $\tau_q,\tau_T$ & Higher-order time derivatives in heat equation & $\tau_q\ge\tau_T$ for thermodynamic admissibility & \cite{Tzou1995} \\
Triple-phase-lag (TPL) & Three phase lags including gradient lag & Third-order temporal terms & Thermal displacement gradient coupling & \cite{Tzou1997} \\
Moore--Gibson--Thompson (MGT) & Third-order heat equation & $\tau\ddot{q}+\dot{q}=-(k/\rho c)\nabla(\dot{T}+\tau\ddot{T})$ & Thermal inertia; acceleration-level flux terms & \cite{Quintanilla2019} \\
