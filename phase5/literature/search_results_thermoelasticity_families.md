## Energy Flux Derivation Sources Needed

For the 8 unresolved energy flux derivations (LS, DPL, TPL, MGT, MDD, GN-II, GN-III, nonlocal), the following primary sources are needed:

1. **LS energy flux:** Derive from qi + τ₀q̇ᵢ = −kT,ᵢ combined with energy balance ρĖ = σ:∇v − ∇·q
2. **DPL energy flux:** From Tzou constitutive law q(t+τq) = −k∇T(t+τT)
3. **TPL energy flux:** From three-phase-lag law with thermal displacement gradient
4. **MGT energy flux:** From third-order heat equation τq̈ + q̇ = −(k/ρc)∇(Ṫ + τṪ̈)
5. **MDD energy flux:** From convolution-form heat flux with kernel
6. **GN-II energy flux:** From entropy flux h = ∂ψ/∂∇α (thermal displacement gradient)
7. **GN-III energy flux:** Mixed type I/II flux
8. **Nonlocal energy flux:** Integral-form flux with Eringen kernel modification of divergence theorem
