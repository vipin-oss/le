\section{Equation Verification Register}
\label{app:equation_register}

This appendix records equation claims and their audit status. Legacy status labels from earlier manuscript work are not accepted as verification in version 1.1 unless the present register explicitly identifies a derivation performed here. No comprehensive primary-source equation audit has been completed.

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{DERIVED:} Algebra checked directly from displayed governing equations in this revision.
\item \textbf{CONDITIONAL:} Valid only under the stated continuum, constitutive, or boundary assumptions.
\item \textbf{PRIMARY-SOURCE-CHECKED:} Checked against the identified primary source; this does not assert an independent derivation.
\item \textbf{SECONDARY-SOURCE:} Supported only by a named secondary source; not equivalent to primary verification.
\item \textbf{UNVERIFIED:} Not checked against a primary source or independently derived in this phase.
\item \textbf{OPEN:} Required derivation or source evidence is absent; not a canonical result.
\end{itemize}

Historical statuses and source notes are not evidence of present verification. The scientific-audit report identifies the limited equations derived in this phase.

\subsection{Open Derivations Requiring Full-Text Verification}

The following model families require a dedicated claim-level source and derivation audit before an energy-flux formula is asserted: Lord--Shulman; Green--Lindsay; Green--Naghdi types II and III; dual- and triple-phase-lag; Moore--Gibson--Thompson; memory-dependent; and nonlocal/gradient models. The full texts, exact constitutive conventions, free energies, external-field power terms, boundary conditions, and evolution laws must be checked for each case. The citations and formulae in the retained working appendix are not evidence that such verification has occurred in this phase.

These model-specific flux derivations remain open; legacy Appendix E/Sec. 10 questions identify work items, not canonical formulae.
