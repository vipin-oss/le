The entries below are proposed reproducibility targets, not reported computational results. No benchmark was executed in this revision. In particular, the inherited “VERIFIED” labels and published numerical values have been removed; a mathematical expectation is not a reproduced benchmark.

\subsection{BM-1: Classical elastic interface}

\textbf{Problem:} Plane P-wave incident on a perfectly bonded interface between two elastic half-spaces.

\textbf{Governing equations:} Navier equations for the stated isotropic elastic model in each medium. Specify densities, Lam\'e moduli, incidence angle, normal orientation, and amplitude normalization before computing coefficients.

\textbf{Expected check:} For a lossless passive interface, evaluate the complete time-averaged normal flux and verify conservation after all propagating channels and any relevant cross terms are included. The shorthand $R_P+T_P=1$ is conditional on a single uncoupled channel and consistent flux normalization.

\textbf{Status:} SPECIFIED, NOT EXECUTED. No numerical value is asserted.
