\section{Figure Source and Audit Status}
\label{app:figures}

The eighteen figure sources are editable SVG files in \texttt{../figures/source/}; all eighteen requested PDF renditions have now been generated by the documented vector-conversion script. The SVGs parse as XML, and the generated PDFs contain vector drawing operators and extractable text. A contact sheet was reviewed for gross placement/content issues, but the figures have not been inspected at final manuscript size or against a compiled v1.1 PDF. Scientific-content and source checks remain incomplete. The CSV register at \texttt{../reports/figure-audit.csv} records source-level observations and remaining status per figure.
