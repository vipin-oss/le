## Candidate 1 — Physics Reports (Elsevier), invitation-only review route

- Guide for authors: https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/physics-reports/publish/guide-for-authors
- Recorded from the excerpt: the journal publishes invited reviews; a proposal of no more than 1000 words is sent to the Editorial Office with a named editor; the target length for Rapid Reviews is about 20 pages, and regular reviews are described as more than 50 journal pages; the abstract for accepted articles does not exceed 250 words.
- Secondary summary (not an official source; do not cite): https://manusights.com/blog/physics-reports-submission-guide
- Fit: the scope (multiphysics wave models) matches the journal's review remit, but the manuscript is 123 pages in the current build, so a regular review would require a proposal that defines a narrower scope. The Rapid Review route (about 20 pages) would require a new, shorter manuscript.
- Open for author: whether to pursue an invited regular review, and which editor to address.
