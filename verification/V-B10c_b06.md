*Hyperbolic two-temperature (§11.3).*

1. DRV-0172 Prasad & R. Kumar 2020, *J. Thermal Stresses* (online 2020-08-24; no volume/pages in the copy read), DOI `10.1080/01495739.2020.1806764`. Eq (5) prints the hyperbolic two-temperature relation: second time derivative of the conductive temperature minus second time derivative of the thermodynamic temperature equals the hyperbolic parameter (≥ 0) times the Laplacian of the conductive temperature. LS heat equation (3) and motion (4) printed. This is the only paper in the batch that carries a **named stability criterion**: plane waves are analysed with **Whitham's criteria**, and the conclusions state that 2THT and LS plane waves are *always stable* in Whitham's sense. No Routh–Hurwitz or semigroup argument. Tag `PRIMARY-SOURCE-CHECKED`, read level FULL.

Eq (38)'s text layer is garbled, so the arrangement of terms is **not** transcribed from here; the clean printed form is quoted from DRV-0172 eq (5). Tag `PARTIAL - PRIMARY SOURCE, eq (38) not legible`.
