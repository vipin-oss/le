**Result.**

*Two-temperature block — all three belong in §11.3, not §4.12.*

1. **DRV-0177 Hobiny, Abbas & Marin 2022**, *Mathematics* **10**(1), 121, DOI `10.3390/math10010121`. **This is the journal version of the B06 Research Square preprint DRV-0176.** Eqs (1)–(5): motion, carrier density, LS-type heat conduction in the conductive temperature with relaxation on the capacity and coupling group, the hyperbolic two-temperature relation at (4) (two second time derivatives, Laplacian coupling), stress. Eq (4) is the same relation as DRV-0172 eq (5), so **§11.3 now has a peer-reviewed citation and the preprint can be dropped from the plan entirely**; the B06 row has been annotated to say so.
2. **DRV-0178 Bassiouny 2021**, *Appl. Math. Inf. Sci.* **15**(1), 23–29, DOI `10.18576/amis/150104`. Heat eq (4): LS-type operator on the capacity and on the coupling term, with a **Caputo fractional derivative acting on the STRAIN COUPLING term** — a hybrid that none of §4.11's routes covers. Hyperbolic relation (9), and the elliptic relation it replaces at (8). Two consequences: the same closure as DRV-0172 is printed with the two sides in the **opposite order**, so the manuscript must fix a sign convention for the two-temperature parameter before tabulating; and a fractional operator on the coupling term is not "fractional heat conduction".
3. **DRV-0179 R. Kumar & T. Kansal 2017**, *Comput. Methods Sci. Technol.* **23**(1), 43–56, DOI `10.12921/cmst.2016.0000036`. Filed in the two-temperature folder but it is **not** a two-temperature paper: it is a reflection/refraction study at an elastic / magneto-thermoelastic-diffusive-void interface. Heat eq (13) is LS-type with relaxation also on the void and concentration terms; the ordering `τ₁ ≥ τ₀ ≥ 0` is printed for both thermal and diffusion lags. **It prints an exact conservation identity at (80)** — reflected ratios plus refracted diagonal ratios plus an interaction term `ERR` defined at (79) — which is the §10.5 structure. This **corrects a B06 statement of mine**: I had recorded eq (6.14) of DRV-0040 as the only printed unity identity in this literature; there is a second one, and unlike (6.14) it names the interaction energy explicitly. Own group ⇒ neutral wording, `CONDITIONAL`, no table numbers adopted.

*Three-phase-lag block — the manuscript's own T2 truncation has printed precedent.*

4. **DRV-0182 Singh, Pal (Sarkar) & Barman 2019**, *J. Thermal Stresses* **42**(7), 874–889, DOI `10.1080/01495739.2019.1602015`. Flux law (8): `(1 + τ_q∂_t + (τ_q²/2)∂²_t)q = −k(1+τ_θ∂_t)∇θ̇ − k*(1+τ_v∂_t)∇θ`; (9) the memory-dependent form; (11)–(12) the heat equations; ordering `0 ≤ τ_v < τ_θ < τ_q` printed and attributed to Roy Choudhuri; LS, GN-III and DPL degenerations printed. **This is the strongest printed precedent for §4.8's second-order truncation** — the `τ_q²/2` coefficient appears with only first-order `τ_θ`, `τ_v`, exactly the combination the manuscript uses.
5. **DRV-0185 Das, Lahiri, Sarkar & Basu 2008**, *Comput. Math. Appl.* **56**, 2795–2805, DOI `10.1016/j.camwa.2008.05.042`. Heat conduction (2) in the `k*`-type no-dissipation form, with the printed statement that the system is hyperbolic so both fields travel at finite speed. **No energy ratios anywhere in the paper** (a full-text grep finds "energy" only in a cited title) — amplitude ratios only, for isothermal and insulated boundaries. It may be cited in §9.3/§9.6 and must **not** be cited in §9.7.
6. **DRV-0186 Biswas 2019**, *Mech. Based Des. Struct. Mach.* **47**(3), 302–318, DOI `10.1080/15397734.2018.1548968`. Orthotropic TPL heat equation in operator form (28) and its memory-dependent form (29), again with the `τ_q²/2` coefficient and with the **1/ω normalisation** on each memory integral (the Wang–Li 2011 convention, i.e. manuscript eq (6.7)'s convention). Conductivity is a **tensor** (`K₁, K₃`, starred pair), so any scalar-`K` sentence in §4.8 is a special case of this paper. No ordering, no admissibility condition.
7. **DRV-0187 Singh, Pal (Sarkar) & Barman 2020**, *J. Thermal Stresses* (online 2020-06-08; no volume/pages in the copy), DOI `10.1080/01495739.2020.1770642`. Prints the **boundedness constraint on the memory kernel**, `0 ≤ K(t−n) < 1`, which §6.4 currently states only descriptively — so the constraint becomes citable rather than asserted. TPL flux law in pure operator form (10), heat equation (11) with the `τ_q²/2` term, and a separate viscoelastic relaxation function with its own parameter restrictions.
8. **DRV-0188 Bayones, Mondal, Abo-Dahab & Kilany 2021**, *Mech. Based Des. Struct. Mach.* (online 2021-04-21), DOI `10.1080/15397734.2021.1901735`. Eringen gradient form (6) applied to the stress (21) **and** to the inertia term (20); memory-dependent LS law (12)–(13); memory-dependent TPL flux law (14) with the `τ_q²/2` term; heat eqs (15), (22). This is a **third nonlocal placement** in the literature — §7 currently describes only "operator on the stress", so §7 must say the literature uses at least three placements.
9. **DRV-0189 Tiwari & Ravi Kumar 2021**, *Eur. J. Mech. A/Solids* **88**, 104235, DOI `10.1016/j.euromechsol.2021.104235`. Nonlocal constitutive (1) with the operator also on the inertia (2); TPL heat conduction (3) with the `τ_q²/2` term, attributed to Roy Choudhuri. **Prints a tensor-form thermodynamic compatibility condition**: `K + (τ_v − τ_q)K*` and `τ_T K − (τ_q/2)(K + τ_v K*)` positive semi-definite, attributed to Chiriţă et al. (2016). That is a second admissibility statement for the T2 truncation, complementary to the scalar Quintanilla window already verified by Routh–Hurwitz in `verification/`, and it must be cited alongside it — with the note that it is imported, not derived.
