# V-B10d_t3_2 — Tier-3 batch 2 (35 files): title page + printed DOI only

**Task.** Second tier-3 batch, `DRV-0062 → DRV-0104` (35 files). Two fields per file — title page and printed DOI — plus matching against `LITERATURE_BASE.csv` by DOI first, then author+year+journal. `AMBIGUOUS` for name-only matches. No tier-3 claim enters the manuscript; the base is not extended. `read_level = METADATA-ONLY`.

**Method.** All 35 downloaded by id, md5 taken, page 1 extracted with `pypdf` (`strict=False`); where page 1 was a cover/watermark page (ResearchGate, HAL, Taylor & Francis front matter) the **article** title page was read instead (pages 2–3). Matching: exact DOI, then title similarity with `difflib`, then a surname and year check on the base.

**Result.** **Two matches, and both are exact.** `DRV-0062` (N. Sarkar, D. Ghosh & A. Lahiri, *Mech. Adv. Mater. Struct.* 2019) matches **LIT-0291** by printed DOI `10.1080/15376494.2018.1432784`, and the titles agree at similarity 1.00. `DRV-0063` (D. S. Chandrasekharaiah, *Appl. Mech. Rev.* **51**(12) Part 1, 1998, from p. 705) matches **LIT-0039** by author+year+title at similarity 1.00; the scanned page prints no DOI, while the base holds `10.1115/1.3098984`, so the match is recorded as author+year+title and the DOI is attributed to the base, not to the page. The remaining 33 files match nothing: printed DOIs are absent from the base, and the closest titles (0.68 for `DRV-0101` against LIT-0275, 0.73 for `DRV-0103` against LIT-0422 — a 1987 elastic-plate-with-voids paper, a different work) are different papers.

**This batch is mostly materials-and-structures engineering, not thermoelasticity.** Twenty of the 35 are CNT-reinforced magneto-electro-elastic plate/beam/sandwich vibration and buckling papers (Hung 2023, Mohammadimehr 2018, Amini 2019, Bamdad 2019, Vinyas 2018/2020, Arefi 2018, Mahesh 2021, Lakshmikanthan 2021); three are pure fractional-calculus boundary-value papers (AIMS Mathematics 2023 ×2, Fractal and Fractional 2023) with no thermoelastic content; one is a foundry/metal-matrix composite vibration paper; one is a health-economics-free quasicrystal review (Janssen & de Boissieu 2014). The thermoelastic core of the batch is the Chandrasekharaiah review, Sarkar's two-temperature MDD paper, Vashishth & Sukhija ×2, Sharma & Pathania 2005, Singh & Tomar 2007 and four flexomagnetic/flexoelectric wave papers (Biswas & Sahu ×3, Hrytsyna 2023, Yang 2017). All of it is metadata only; **nothing here was read for content**.

It is recorded `UNREADABLE` with no title, author or year guessed. The file name suggests a 2002 work by Slaughter, which is recorded nowhere as fact.
