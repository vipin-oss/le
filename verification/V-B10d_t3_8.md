# V-B10d_t3_8 — Tier-3 batch 8 (29 files) and close of tier 3

**Task.** Last tier-3 batch, `DRV-0339 → DRV-0370` (29 files; the roster skips `DRV-0340`, `0341`, `0363`). Title page and printed DOI only; DOI-first match against `LITERATURE_BASE.csv`, then against DOIs already recorded in the tier-3 file, then author+year+journal. No tier-3 claim enters the manuscript; base not extended.

**Method.** 29 files downloaded by id (all success), copied to `/tmp`, first page extracted with `pypdf`; where the header was a publisher cover or the title was split across a two-column line, page index 1–2 was re-read with PyMuPDF (`DRV-0342` T&F cover → p.2; `DRV-0348` IOPscience cover → p.2; `DRV-0370` → journal/volume line). Volume and page strings taken from the printed running head, not from the file name.

**Result.** **One DOI match and two within-library duplicates.**

* `DRV-0366` → **LIT-0495** — Kumawat & Vishwakarma, circumferential SH wave in a piezo-reinforced composite with imperfect interface, *Appl. Math. Model.* **123** (2023) 311–331.
* `DRV-0347` → duplicate of `DRV-0310` (batch 7) — same Abouelregal/Marin/Foul/Askar *Bound. Value Probl.* 2025:10 paper, downloaded twice under different names.
* `DRV-0367` → duplicate of `DRV-0298` (batch 6) — the fractional Kelvin–Voigt circumferential-wave paper, `10.1016/j.apm.2020.06.077`.

**Two rows are review copies, not published articles, and are recorded as such.** `DRV-0360` carries a "For Review Only" watermark and a manuscript ID (`cjp-2015-0782.R1`) but no volume or DOI; whether it was ever published in *Canad. J. Phys.* was **not** established, so it has no DOI in the metadata file. `DRV-0364` is an accepted-manuscript PDF with no volume or DOI on the page. Its file name encodes `10.1080/15376494.2016.1255826`, but that string is **not printed on the page** and is therefore not entered as `doi_printed` — it is noted in `notes` as unverified. Neither may be cited as a published paper on the strength of this file.

**Two rows have no printed DOI at all.** `DRV-0343` (Kumar, Devi & Sharma, *AAM* **12**(2) 2017, Article 19) and `DRV-0348` (Hu, Wang & Ding, *Rep. Prog. Phys.* **63** (2000) 1–39, PII printed). Both identified from the title page; neither matched a base row.

**Content notes that matter more than the match count.**
- **Seven *Crystals* papers, 2022–2023** (`DRV-0353`–`0359`), all from the Lotfy/El-Bary group: photo-thermoelastic semiconductor models with stochastic fields, moisture diffusivity, Hall current, microelongation, hyperbolic two-temperature. They are near-identical model variants of one another. If any is used, one should be cited, not the set, and the manuscript should not imply a literature of independent confirmations where the same group is iterating on its own model.
- **Three imperfect-interface waveguide papers** (`DRV-0342` hollow piezo-composite cylinder, `DRV-0366` piezo-reinforced composite, `DRV-0370` piezoelectric cylinder in a piezomagnetic matrix). With tier-2's four-parameter stiffness family and `DRV-0336`, §9.3/§9.8 now have a well-populated printed literature on interface conditions in cylindrical geometry — this is the one area where the library is genuinely deep.
- **Two phase-field thermal-shock fracture papers** (`DRV-0368`, `DRV-0369`). Different method family from the review; relevant only if §12 discusses thermal-shock cracking as an application.
- **`DRV-0348` is the missing general reference for the quasicrystal sub-corpus.** Hu, Wang & Ding's *Rep. Prog. Phys.* review covers symmetry groups, property tensors, elasticity and dislocations — the canonical source for the phonon/phason elastic framework the eight quasicrystal files of batch 6 assume. Recorded metadata-only; **not read**, so it cannot yet be cited for any specific equation.
- **Two files are clearly outside the review.** `DRV-0344` is a pure operator-theory paper (Berezin numbers, *Baku Math. J.*) and `DRV-0350` is rheology of waste activated sludge (*Sci. Total Environ.*). They are the clearest evidence yet that the folder is personal working storage.
