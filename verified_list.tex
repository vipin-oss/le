\subsection{Verified equation register: assumptions and limits}
\label{sec:12.verified_register}

The displays below were verified either symbolically (VERIFIED-SYMBOLIC) or against the
primary source (VERIFIED-SOURCE) in this revision. For each display, the assumptions
under which it holds and the limits beyond which it does not apply are stated. Conformance
statements are restricted to the displayed equations and the cited sources; they are not
convergence, stability, or source-transcription results beyond the stated scope.
